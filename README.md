# Cursor trampoline

Drop your cursor onto a tiny trampoline and land it in a pile of autumn leaves.

Click anywhere above the trampoline. The cursor falls from that spot, straight down or at an angle depending on where you clicked, and the trampoline shoots it toward the pile. A dashed line shows the whole flight before you let go, so you can aim. Land in the leaves and they fly up; miss and the cursor sticks in the ground.

Live: https://mudranagda.github.io/cursor-trampoline/

One HTML file, plain canvas, no libraries. It began as the footer of [mudranagda.com](https://mudranagda.com).
